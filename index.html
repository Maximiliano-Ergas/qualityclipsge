QualityClipsGE

A private desktop tool that turns live-stream recordings into short vertical clips and publishes them to the operator's own accounts.

What it does

  • Finds the most engaging moments in a live-stream recording.
  • Cuts them into short vertical (9:16) clips with captions.
  • Uploads the clips to the operator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

QualityClipsGE runs on the operator's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.
